Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, straightforward answers before you book.

What happens during the Free Evaluation?

We meet with you and your dog either at our training facility or virtually. We assess your dog's temperament, discuss your lifestyle goals, pinpoint behavioral triggers, and give you an honest appraisal of the recommended training path with zero pressure.

Do you use shock collars or harsh punishment?

No. We prioritize positive reinforcement, marker training, and clear communication. If modern electronic or balanced communication tools are ever requested for off-leash distance recall, they are introduced at humane sensation levels only with full owner consent and proper conditioning.

How much does dog training cost?

Because every dog and behavior issue is unique, pricing varies based on whether private lessons, day-school, or board and train is most effective. Private lesson packages start at $450, while immersive multi-week programs range from $1,800 to $3,200. Transparent pricing is provided upfront during your evaluation.

What is your refund and satisfaction policy?

We stand behind our work. While live animals cannot be subject to identical manufactured goods returns, if your dog does not demonstrate significant progress according to the agreed training goals, we provide free additional one-on-one sessions until those milestones are reached.
